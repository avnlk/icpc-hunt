# Regional selection rules (as published by the organisers)

Sources: the Amritapuri "onsite selection process" page and the Kanpur team-selection PDF. Re-check them; the
organisers can change them, and values such as X, Y and Z are announced after the preliminary round.

## Amritapuri
- 380 slots: 360 general + 20 women-only.
- Drop teams with 0 solved, rank the rest.
- Ranks 1-50: selected outright (no per-college limit).
- Ranks 51-100: up to **X** teams per college (counting those already selected).
- Ranks 101-140: up to **Y** teams per college (X > Y).
- Ranks 141 onward: rounds of one team per college that has none selected yet; then rounds of the next-best
  team per college, until the 360 slots are full.
- Women-only slots: all-women teams not already selected, at most **Z** per college.

## Kanpur
- 240 slots: 235 general + 5 women-only.
- Drop teams with 0 solved, rank the rest.
- Top 30 selected outright.
- Then rounds of the best remaining team per college, until the 235 general slots are full.
- Women-only slots filled the same way from all-women teams.

## What this means in practice
- After the open / capped bands, **each round gives every college one seat**, so the number of colleges in the
  pool decides how far the rounds get. Fewer colleges (for example through a minimum-solves cutoff, or removals)
  means later rounds are reached.
- A college's second team is only considered in the second round, after every college has had a first.
- Teams register for up to two sites; each site builds its own list from the shared preliminary ranking.

## Not stated in the published text
- Any minimum number of solved problems (only 0-solve teams are dropped).
- The values of X, Y and Z.
- Whether plagiarism removals shrink a college's count or remove the whole college.
